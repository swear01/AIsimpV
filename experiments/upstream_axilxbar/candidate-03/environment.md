1. Replaced rtl/axilxbar.v with a simplified read-only crossbar/error-write stub. 2. The candidate does not instantiate addrdecode, skidbuffer, faxil_slave, or faxil_master; those files may remain unchanged but are unused by this candidate. 3. Write channels accept one write per master and return SLVERR (2'b11); no writes are routed to slaves and M_AXI write outputs are tied off. 4. Read channels support at most one outstanding read per master and at most one master per real slave; no pipelining, linger, or multi-outstanding reads. 5. Address decoding is combinational priority decode, not the registered OPT_BUFFER_DECODER behavior. 6. OPT_LINGER, LGMAXBURST, and OPT_LOWPOWER are ignored except as unused parameters. 7. The original formal checkers and cover properties in axilxbar.v are not instantiated. 8. No new assumptions were added to the SBY script, but the effective formal environment is smaller because the above modules and checkers are not instantiated.
