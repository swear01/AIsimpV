This candidate is a partial, deliberately simplified read-grant-focused replacement for rtl/axilxbar.v. It implements a small read-only crossbar with one outstanding read per master and one master per real slave, plus an error-write stub. The candidate property under FORMAL directly mirrors the original CHECK_MASTER_GRANTS read-grant obligations identified in task.json: if a master has a read grant bit set, the grant is one-hot, srgrant is asserted, srindex matches the selected route, and for real slaves mrgrant/mrindex are reciprocal. It also checks that srgrant implies a nonzero rgrant and that the rrequest error bit is disjoint from real-slave request bits. The original write-grant assertions, full read/write arbitration, skid buffering, addrdecode registration, multiple outstanding transactions, linger behavior, OPT_LOWPOWER zeroing, original cover properties, and the faxil_slave/faxil_master formal checkers are not represented in this candidate. No compilation, simulation, formal proof, or equivalence check was run. This is a hypothesis, not a proof.
