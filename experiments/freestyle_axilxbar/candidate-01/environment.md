Original environment: no additional assumptions; all design inputs are free; reset guards both observed edges. New candidate environment changes: restrict attention to master 0 and slave 0 read traffic; all write inputs are treated as idle/inactive; other masters and other slaves are idle/inactive. The simplified RTL does not require an assumption that M_AXI_RVALID[0], M_AXI_RDATA[0], or M_AXI_RRESP[0] must remain stable while stalled, because the registered hold stage enforces the property internally. The reset guard on the observed edges is unchanged. SLAVE_ADDR, SLAVE_MASK, OPT_LINGER, and LGMAXBURST are kept only for interface compatibility and are not used by the simplified behavior. The property binding is unchanged: clk=S_AXI_ACLK, resetn=S_AXI_ARESETN, rvalid=S_AXI_RVALID[0], rready=S_AXI_RREADY[0], rdata=S_AXI_RDATA[0+:32], rresp=S_AXI_RRESP[0+:2].
